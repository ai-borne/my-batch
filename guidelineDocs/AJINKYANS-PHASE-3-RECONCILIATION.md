# Phase 3 reconciliation workflow

## Daily during collections

1. A Coordinator compares each submitted UTR, amount, and payment date with the designated collection account.
2. Mark the claim **Verified**, **Rejected**, or **Under Review** with a clarification note. Do not copy UTRs or evidence into external chat messages.
3. Use **Rebuild fund summary** after completing the review queue and compare the aggregate with the account statement.
4. Record vendor expenses as drafts. A Coordinator approves an expense only after checking its receipt; approval updates the public aggregate.
5. Export the CSV ledger and retain it in the Coordinator-controlled reconciliation location.

## Weekly after the reunion

1. Repeat the account-statement-to-ledger comparison.
2. Resolve every submitted or under-review claim and every expense draft.
3. Rebuild the summary, export the final ledger, and preserve it with the underlying account statement and receipts.

The public fund view contains aggregate totals and approved expenses only. Payment claims, UTRs, screenshots, drafts, rejected expenses, and audit events remain Coordinator-only.
